## 1. Executive summary

Overall the goal-7 work is **unusually trustworthy for an AI+1-user project with no hydrologist**: the load-bearing numbers I attacked (structure table, land/economics tables, Manning capacity, screening arithmetic, GIS overlay) all reproduce from the official PDFs and from first principles, the pipeline rebuilds **byte-identical**, and the neutrality/legal framing rules are genuinely enforced by tests. The three things to fix first:

1. **Stale "2.5–4.8 ม." in the academic TLDR** (builder literal) contradicting the corrected "3.2–4.8" used everywhere else — the single most visible inconsistency in the flagship report (G1).
2. **`eia_profile()` loop bug**: the Nakhon Nayok reach statistics are computed over km 23–58 only, so `canonical → eia_profile.nn_reach.ground_min_m` = 1.95 is wrong (true = 1.47). Headline 3.2–4.8 survives by luck; no report prints the wrong field (G2).
3. **Undisclosed source conflict**: the 2569 report itself contradicts on end water level (+1.50 p.151 vs +2.00 p.184); the team knows (GOAL7_PLAN §23) but no report tells the reader, although the profile tail and the options screening assume +1.50 (G4).

Three things that held up under attack: (a) Table 2.3.2-3 parsing — 15 rows spot-checked against the raw PDF text of two editions, **zero discrepancies**, and the "q_in undefined except แม่น้ำใน 28.05 = PK-AT-4 q10" claim is correct; (b) the Manning/self-consistency chain — EIA's A=504 m², V=1.20 m/s, n=0.016, S=1:25,000 reproduce a bottom width of exactly 51 m and Q≈605–608 m³/s at D=7, z=3; (c) the GIS overlay — 16.8 km/46.8% reproduced, and **robust to ±300 m alignment perturbation** (16.4–17.1 km / 45.8–47.5%); the 3-basin containment claim verified independently against the live ONWR 22-basin layer.

Definition of done confirmed: pytest **85/85 pass**; integrity **MISMATCH 2** (both pre-existing in `data/22_goal6_network`), MISSING 37 (expected, disk-only). I stopped after one verification round.
